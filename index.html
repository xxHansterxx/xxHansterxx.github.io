About Me

Hi, I'm Hannah, a recent graduate with a degree in AI and Robotics from Staffordshire University. My primary interests are embedded systems and robotics. Alongside my passion for programming, I enjoy staying active through inline hockey, aqua aerobics and hiking whenever the weather allows. Since childhood I also have an interest in themed entertainment although it took until I was 13 to ride my first major roller coaster which happened to be Stealth at Thorpe Park

My interest in engineering began at the age of 13 when I took part in the FIRST Tech Challenge, where I was introduced to the world of robotics. The following year, I returned as a mentor to support the next group of competitors. Although the competition focused on designing and building robots rather than programming them, it sparked my curiosity with engineering and inspired me to pursue a career in Aerospace Engineering.

During college, my career ambitions began to shift after being introduced to programming by a fellow student. I quickly discovered the world of programming and that I really enjoyed it. This led me to apply for degrees in either AI and Robotics or Aerospace Engineering. changing career path at 18 to a programming focused career felt like a bold move, but it soon proved to be the right decision.

Studying AI and Robotics at Staffordshire University challenged me from the very beginning. Like many students, I experienced moments of doubt during my first year, but with persistence I developed both my confidence and technical abilities. By my second year, I had established a solid foundation in programming and discovered embedded programming, which quickly became my favourite area of study. Working with C and microcontrollers showed me how software can directly interact with hardware to solve problems, and it remains the area of programming I am most passionate about.

Alongside my studies, I've continued to explore areas of technology that interest me and enjoy learning new skills through personal projects. I'm particularly motivated by opportunities to solve practical problems, understand how systems work at a low level and continuously improve my programming ability. My interest in themed entertainment has also inspired many of the ideas behind my personal projects as can be seen in my Final Year Project on the Learn Embedded page.

Today, I am excited to begin my career as an embedded programmer and hope this website documents my journey that shapes my transition from student to professional programmer.

Why I'm Building This Website

As I began preparing for my career in software engineering, I made a list of the skills and technologies that I wanted to develop. Some were completely new to me, while others were areas where I wanted to further my understanding. Rather than treating each topic as a separate task, I decided to combine them into one long-term project that would allow me to learn by building something meaningful.

This website is that project. It serves as both a learning platform and a record of my progress as I continue to develop my technical skills. Throughout the site, you'll find projects, experiments and notes covering the technologies and concepts I'm exploring, from web development fundamentals such as semantic HTML to embedded systems programming and other areas of software engineering.

As with any learning journey, not everything will be perfect. Some solutions may not be the best way t do it but as I learn more and projects grow, each section should improve. Rather than hiding mistakes, I want this website to reflect the reality of learning, experimenting, refining ideas and continuously improving.

I am excited to share my progress through this website and hope you'll enjoy following my journey as I continue to learn, build and develop my skills. Thank you for taking the time to visit and I look forward to seeing where this journey takes me.
